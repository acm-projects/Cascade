## AUDIO (measured: librosa + spectrograms)
- Everything is mastered to about -14 LUFS (platform loudness).
- Nature/earth pieces (editing_09/15/17/18, broll_01): low, dark spectrum (median centroid 500–1800 Hz), slow tempos (66–80 BPM feel), long sustained pads/piano notes over a deep sub pedal (~55–65 Hz), real nature ambience (water, birds). Very little percussion.
- MUSIC DROPS TO SILENCE right before the key line/payoff (editing_09 at ~19s, editing_11 several times, editing_04 at 3.5s and 11.2s). Silence → impact is the main emotional tool.
- editing_12 / end_21: SUB-BOOM DOWNSWEEPS (pitch-falling 808 thumps) land exactly on cuts; short silence right before each.
- editing_03: broadband whoosh streaks on almost every cut; speech stays on top, music ducked under it.
- editing_11: music sections START on the cut (cuts_on_beat 0.48) and stop dead between "Earth is ___" lines.
- end_21: rising plucked arpeggio with a sub drop under each note, resolves to a held chord (logo sting).
- Cut sync to beat: 0.3–0.5 for talk pieces (cuts follow SPEECH, not music); up to 0.7–1.0 for music-led montage. → In a podcast clip the edit must follow the sentence boundaries; music is arranged around speech.

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR CASCADE (synthesis)
1. CLEAN DIALOGUE FIRST. Edit on sentence and word boundaries; remove ums, false starts and dead air; never cut inside a word; keep one speaker at a time (Q → A), drop the other mic's interjections; equal-power 15–30 ms audio crossfades at every join so nothing clicks or clips. Room tone under the joins.
2. THREE-ACT ARC for a cause clip: (a) HOOK — Earth zoom / text-mask word filled with rainforest; (b) THE ANSWER — speaker with giant word behind them, outline reveal, context-colored keywords; out-of-place b-roll inserts (landfill, fire, chainsaw vs. untouched canopy); (c) AWAKENING — music cuts to silence, one sentence in small serif on black/nature, then a sub-boom + the call to action, then the Cascade splash sting.
3. TEXT SYSTEM: heavy grotesk (Anton/Bebas-like) + light italic serif (Instrument Serif / Playfair italic) pairing; giant word BEHIND the speaker with an outline copy in front; small serif one-phrase lines for emotional beats; keyword color by meaning — water/rivers = #3FB8FF blue, forest/life/hope = #4BE38A green, danger/burn/destroy/loss = #FF3B30 red, money/greed = #F5C542 gold, everything else warm white. Always with a soft dark shadow, kept off the face, inside the safe area.
4. B-ROLL: short (0.6–1.5s) inserts that cut ON a spoken keyword; contrast pairs (canopy ↔ deforestation); slow pushes, never static; occasional 4:3 card with rounded vignette + grain; RGB-split or white-flash transitions sparingly.
5. SILHOUETTES/GLOW: speaker cut-out with colored rim glow (green for hope, red for danger) and glowing white silhouette inserts.
6. GRADE: crushed blacks, teal shadows + warm skin, lifted greens for forest, slight grain + vignette; desaturate to near-B&W for "loss" moments, then bloom color back for "hope".
7. SOUND: dialog on top always (ducked bed −18 dB under speech); dark ambient pad + sub pedal; nature ambience bed (rain/forest/river); sub-boom downsweep on the big cuts; whoosh on transitions only; hard music drop-out before the payoff line. No stock "ding/boing/swoosh" pack sounds.
8. END STAMP (2.5s): water drop → splash ripple → liquid wipe → "Cascade" handwritten stroke-draw → droplets fall off the letters; rising pluck + water splash + sub.
